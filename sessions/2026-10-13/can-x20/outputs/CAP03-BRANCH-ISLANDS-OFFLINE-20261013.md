# Capability #3: bounded x64 short-branch islands, offline

Stage 44 at `/WORKSPACE/.codex/worktrees/translation-core/caps-resume-20261013/caps-team-work/cg-cap03-branch-islands-20261013-44` builds on the exact stage-43 detour planner. When an external 8-bit conditional or unconditional branch cannot reach its original destination from the trampoline, the planner redirects it to a nearby 14-byte absolute-jump island after the trampoline's jump-back. The copied instruction keeps its original length and fallthrough path. Up to seven islands fit the bounded output, corresponding to the maximum number of two-byte branches that can start before the 14-byte overwrite threshold. An unreachable island, overflow, or overlap is refused.
