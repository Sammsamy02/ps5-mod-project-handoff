## 3. Measured findings en route (recorded for the next worker)

1. ld.lld `-r` UND-type merge keeps the FIRST-seen symbol type. With the typed shim after the
   checkpoint writer, the merged sceKernelGetModuleInfo UND became NOTYPE and the Packager
   refused ("Missing exact typed owned import reference"). Link order was fixed to
   import_references -> shim -> module_entry (measured both ways, 2026-10-04).
2. The prospero target emits NO unwind tables for plain C under the frozen flag set, so the
   first minimal build had no .eh_frame at all. The checkpoint unit is compiled with
   `-fasynchronous-unwind-tables` (driver-level addition, recorded in each receipt; the frozen
   import_references.S and the recipe flags are untouched). The composition-3 runtime got its
   .eh_frame from C++ units.
3. The task's "composition-4 = 198 imports" measures the composed object's raw undefined set.
   The PACKAGED composition-4 delta over the frozen 191 is exactly five strong rows
   (sceKernelDlsym, sceKernelGetModuleInfo, sceKernelLoadStartModule, sceKernelStopUnloadModule
   — libkernel.prx; qsort — libSceLibcInternal.prx; measured from
   `worker-composition-4-fix/prx-candidate-composition4-1/supplier-contract.json`); the
   remaining names are linker-provided boundaries (`__dso_handle`, `__eh_frame_*`) and the
   allowed-weak `kernel_mprotect`. The STAGE-PLAN ladder bisects the five real rows.
