## How to read the result (ue4ss log, decoded)

Use the **last** `[zi] SUM` line before you closed the game. Earlier lines (menus, loading) do not count.

| Last line says | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `state=PASS` | PASS. A replaced game mesh came from the mod, and all the new mod objects it uses are loaded. |
| `state=MISSING` | FAIL. A mesh that was loaded needs a mod object that is not there. The mount missed it (or the game's own mesh loaded instead of the mod's). The `f=0:missing` lines name it. |
| `state=PARENT-WRONG` | FAIL. A material points at a different parent than the Mac said (`par=0:<name>`). The Mac rule needs fixing, or the game's own version loaded. |
| `state=PARENT-UNREAD` | Not a verdict. The checker could not read a material's parent (`par=0:none`). That is a reading problem in the checker or runtime, not a wrong prediction. |
| `state=NOT-LOADED-YET` or `state=INCONCLUSIVE` | Not a verdict. Nothing (or not enough) of Leon was loaded. Was Leon on screen? |
| no `[zi]` lines at all | FAIL of the setup: `zzintake` was not loaded (loadlist or size). |

Also:
- `f=0:notloaded` on a line is fine on its own. It means nothing loaded uses that object right now.
- PASS (visual, from you): the checker shows on Leon and the listed Simon parts, and nowhere else.
- A crash, or #7's own FAIL lines, is a FAIL.
- First line `[zi] Z0 v2 n=<N> new=<a> repl=<b>` shows the checker started. Today's file: `n=21 new=13 repl=8`
  (it changes after the re-run).
